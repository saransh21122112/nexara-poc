FAQ

Questions people ask us.

Short answers. If yours is missing, ask us.

Do we need technical skills to use what you build?

No. Your team uses it with short, plain requests. We give you simple steps to get started, and we are happy to walk you through them.

Where do we use it?

You use it inside Claude Code, on your own computer. If you are new to it, our instructions explain what you need and how to set it up.

What is the difference between a plugin, a skill, an MCP server and a tool?

A skill is one focused ability. A plugin bundles several skills for your company. An MCP server connects your AI tools to your systems in a controlled way. A tool is a single-purpose helper with clear inputs and outputs. Not sure? Choose Custom and describe the problem.

What happens to the information we share?

We use it only to understand and build what you asked for. Please remove confidential details from examples. Our privacy page has the full picture.

Who owns what you build for us?

Ownership and usage rights are set out in your order or agreement with us.

Can we change it later?

Yes. Tell us what should change. We prepare the update, show you exactly what is different, and a person reviews it before it reaches you.

How much does it cost, and how long does it take?

It depends on the size of the request. Contact us with a short description and we will tell you before you commit to anything.

Can we see progress while you work?

Yes. After you submit a request you get a reference you can use on the tracking page.

What if it does not work well for us?

Tell us what you saw. We will either fix it or explain clearly why it behaves that way.
