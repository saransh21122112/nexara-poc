How we work

Clear steps, no surprises.

From your first message to something your team is using, you always know where things stand and what happens next.

01

Tell us

Describe the problem and what a good result looks like. A few real examples help, with anything confidential removed. Our assistant can fill in the form from a short description.

02

We design

We turn your request into a clear plan: what it does, what it will never do, and what it needs from you.

03

We verify

Before you see anything, we try it on realistic situations from your world and check the results.

04

Handover

You are invited to your own private repository with simple instructions to start using it.

05

Evolve

When something should change, we prepare the update and show you what is different. A person approves it before it reaches you.

What we need from you

  • A clear description of the problem
  • A few example inputs, anonymised
  • The tools your team already uses
  • A GitHub username for your private repository

What you get

  • Your build in a private repository
  • Plain-language instructions to start
  • A way to see progress while we work
  • A route to ask for changes later

How long it takes depends on the size of the request. We tell you what to expect before we start.
